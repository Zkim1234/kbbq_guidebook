**Guidebook for Korean BBQ**
With the global rise of K-dramas, many viewers have been introduced to the vibrant aspects of Korean culture, one of the most visually enticing being Korean barbecue. Scenes featuring actors enjoying sizzling meat at a grill table have sparked curiosity and inspired international audiences to experience this culinary tradition firsthand.

However, for many first-time visitors to Korean barbecue restaurants, the experience can be both exciting and intimidating. Questions often arise, such as which cut of meat to order, how to cook it properly, and what sauce to pair it with.

This guidebook aims to bridge that gap by offering a friendly yet informative introduction to the art of Korean barbecue. It includes:
•	Explanations of popular meats such as pork belly, marinated short ribs, and beef brisket.
•	Step-by-step guidance on cooking techniques to achieve authentic flavor and texture.
•	Introductions to various dipping sauces and condiments, explaining which combinations best complement each type of meat.
•	Cultural insights and dining tips on how Koreans enjoy barbecue, including social etiquette, side dishes, and the shared dining experience.

Ultimately, this guidebook seeks to help readers not only learn how to enjoy Korean barbecue but also understand why it holds such a cherished place in Korean dining culture.
